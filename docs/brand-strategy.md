# TAVRO BRAND STRATEGY

---

## POSITIONING STATEMENT

**What Tavro Is**

Tavro is the operational intelligence platform for Nigerian hospitality. It combines POS, inventory, payments, staff management, and business intelligence into a single system that gives restaurants, bars, lounges, clubs, and hotels real visibility and control.

**What Tavro Does**

It makes daily operations simple for staff while giving managers and owners real-time visibility into sales, stock, payments, staff activity, and business performance.

**Why It Matters**

Most hospitality businesses operate on incomplete information. They don't see what's really selling. They don't know true margins. They can't track stock accurately. They lose money to theft, waste, and inefficiency. Tavro changes that.

## PRIMARY MARKET

### Geography
**Nigeria first, with regional expansion potential.**

Hospitality is growing fast across Nigeria. Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan are key markets. The market is fragmented (mostly manual or terrible legacy systems).

### Customer Segments

**1. High-end Lagos/Abuja Venues**
- Premium restaurants, lounges, upscale clubs, boutique hotels
- Pain point: Need premium operations management without expensive consulting
- Value: Sophistication, reliability, growth visibility
- Price sensitivity: Medium (willing to pay for quality)

**2. Growing Restaurant Chains**
- Expanding businesses with 2-5+ locations
- Pain point: Multiple branches, inconsistent operations, no group visibility
- Value: Unified control, multi-branch reporting, staff management
- Price sensitivity: Medium (ROI-focused)

**3. Mid-Market Hospitality**
- Established local restaurants, bars, lounges, 3-4 star hotels
- Pain point: Loss of margin, inventory blind spots, staff tracking
- Value: Cost control, profitability visibility, efficiency
- Price sensitivity: Higher (budget-conscious)

**4. Fast-Growing QSR/Casual**
- Emerging concepts, franchise startups, high-volume operations
- Pain point: Speed, accuracy, scaling operations
- Value: Fast service, inventory accuracy, growth readiness
- Price sensitivity: Medium (growth-focused)
