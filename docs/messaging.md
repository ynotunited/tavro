## TONE OF VOICE IN DIFFERENT CONTEXTS

### Marketing Copy (Website, Ads)
**Tone:** Confident, compelling, direct
**Length:** Short, punchy sentences
**Focus:** Outcomes and benefits
> "Stop losing money to invisible waste. Tavro shows you exactly what's costing you profits."

### Product Interface (POS, Dashboard)
**Tone:** Clear, instructive, minimal
**Length:** 2–5 words per label
**Focus:** Action and clarity
> "Add Item" / "Confirm Sale" / "Check Stock"

### Customer Communication (Email, Support)
**Tone:** Professional, helpful, personal
**Length:** Clear, scannable
**Focus:** Solving their specific problem
> "We've added real-time inventory sync. Your stock count updates instantly across all terminals."

### Educational Content (Guides, Onboarding)
**Tone:** Friendly, practical, step-by-step
**Length:** conversational but focused
**Focus:** Teaching without talking down
> "Here's the fastest way to set up your menu. It takes about 10 minutes."

### Sales/Business Development
**Tone:** Confident, consultative, data-driven
**Length:** Specific and evidence-based
**Focus:** ROI and business impact
> "Restaurants using Tavro reduce inventory shrink by an average of 18% in the first 3 months."

## LANGUAGE SPECIFICS

### Words We Use

- **Control, visibility, insight, clarity, simplicity**
- **Real, actual, true, honest**
- **Decision, action, result, outcome**
- **Integration, connected, unified, seamless**
- **Grow, expand, scale, advance**

### Words We Avoid

❌ Leverage, synergize, paradigm shift, next-gen
❌ Disruptive, revolutionary, game-changing (overused)
❌ Seamless, magical, intelligent (when not backed by reality)
❌ Solutions, platforms, ecosystems (generic)
❌ Industry-leading, best-in-class (unverifiable)
❌ Blockchain, AI, machine learning (unless specifically relevant)

### Number Usage

**Use specific numbers:**
> "18% reduction in shrink" (specific, credible)

**Not vague percentages:**
> "Significantly reduce waste" (too vague)

**Use real currency:**
> "Save ₦2.5M monthly on inventory waste"

**Not:**
> "Save thousands"

## MESSAGING FRAMEWORK FOR NEW PRODUCTS

When launching new features or products, follow this formula:

1. **Problem:** What's the user struggling with?
2. **Proof:** How does Tavro solve it?
3. **Outcome:** What's the real benefit?
4. **Action:** What should they do?

**Example:**

> *Problem:* "You can't track staff performance across multiple locations."
> 
> *Proof:* "Tavro now gives you unified staff performance dashboards for all your venues."
> 
> *Outcome:* "You see who's most efficient, identify training opportunities, and make fair compensation decisions."
> 
> *Action:* "Access your new staff dashboard in Analytics → Team Performance."
